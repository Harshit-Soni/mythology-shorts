# Curses in the Mahabharata: catalogue

Every notable *shaapa* (curse) in the Mahabharata, in the order the epic tells it, for picking S01 episodes. Locations are given by **parva and story** (upakhyana / sub-parva); exact chapter numbers are confirmed in each episode's `01-research.md` (Ganguli and the BORI critical edition number chapters differently).

**Source labels**
- **Epic**: in the Mahabharata itself.
- **Epic (frame)**: in the Mahabharata, but told as a story inside a story (often by a sage to the Pandavas).
- **Later / folk**: not in the Mahabharata; from a later Purana, play, regional or TV retelling. Never presented as scripture (CLAUDE.md rule 1).

**Fit** (for this pipeline): ★★★ few characters, reuses the library, one clear twist · ★★ needs new cast or a set · ★ hard (transformation, crowds, cosmic scale), or better in the illustrated version.

Status: 🎬 made (see `episodes/INDEX.md` for publish state) · 📋 in the S01 plan · 💡 new candidate.

## Often told as Mahabharata, but not in it

| Story | Where it really comes from | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Durvasa curses Shakuntala so Dushyanta forgets her | Kalidasa's play *Abhijnana Shakuntalam*. In the Mahabharata, Dushyanta remembers and denies her on purpose | A "TV vs scripture" episode |
| Bhudevi curses Karna | Later retellings | Mention only as "in some tellings" |
| Draupadi curses dogs (and others) | Folk tales | Don't use as scripture |
| Ashwatthama is cursed "forever" | Popular retelling; the epic says 3,000 years | Correct it on screen; that is a hook in itself |
