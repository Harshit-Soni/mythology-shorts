# Series 1: Shraap / Curses That Shaped the Mahabharata
**Hindi title:** शाप: जिन्होंने महाभारत लिख दी *(working title)*

## Why this series
- **Self-contained:** each curse is a complete 45–60 s story with a built-in twist (a crime, then a curse, then the consequence).
- **Built for binge-watching:** the curses connect to each other, so every episode ends by pointing to the next.
- **Lesser-known angle:** most people know the war, but few know that a sage's curse is the reason Vidura exists, or why Arjuna spent a year as a woman.
- **Character reuse:** the same cast (Krishna, Arjuna, Karna, Bhishma...) recurs, so the asset library grows quickly and later episodes are cheaper to make.

## Recurring series elements
- Opening sting: a folk-art border closes in, and the word **शाप / CURSED** is stamped in gold (about 1 s).
- Closing line format: *"And this curse led to another..."* plus a glimpse of the next episode's character.
