# Series 3: Chiranjeevi, the ones who never died
**Hindi title:** चिरंजीवी: जो कभी नहीं मरे *(working title)*

**Status (2026-11-26):** planned (owner's choice, option a). Nothing researched yet.

## Why this series
- **One question per episode:** *why can't he die?* Each answer is a complete story with a twist (a boon, a promise, a reward, or a curse).
- **Everyone knows the list; few know the reasons.** Strong "still alive?" hooks, answered honestly.
- **Beyond the Mahabharata:** the Ramayana and the Puranas, as the owner wanted.
- **A bridge to Shraap:** most live on by a boon; Ashwatthama lives on by a curse.

## The list, and an accuracy rule
- The well-known verse names seven: Ashwatthama, Bali, Vyasa, Hanuman, Vibhishana, Kripa, Parashurama. Markandeya is often added as an eighth.
- **Where the verse comes from must be traced in research.** It's believed to be a later devotional verse, not a line of the critical Mahabharata: if so, say "the traditional verse", never "the Mahabharata says".
- **"Chiranjeevi" = long-lived**, often until the end of the present world-age (kalpa), not literally immortal. Say what each text says, episode by episode.

## Ashwatthama stays in Shraap (option a)
S01E08 promises "Next season on Shraap: Ashwatthama". So he is **not** an episode here; he opens Shraap Season 2 (`series/S02-shraap-season-2/series.md`). This series tells the other seven, and its last episode points to him.
