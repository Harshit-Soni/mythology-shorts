# Research: S01E04

**Main source:** K. M. Ganguli's public-domain translation (vulgate), read on sacred-texts.com on 2026-11-18:
- **Adi Parva §102:** the abduction.
- **Udyoga Parva §174–195:** the *Ambopakhyana*, told by Bhishma himself to Duryodhana, explaining why he won't fight Shikhandin.
- **Bhishma Parva §108–121:** the fall.
- **Ashvamedhika Parva §81:** the Vasus' curse.

**Critical edition:** unlike E03's Urvashi curse, the Amba story **is in the BORI critical edition** (the Ambopakhyana, Udyoga ≈5.170–193; Vedapath shows ch. 188 with Rudra's boon and Amba's self-immolation, [Vedapath 5.188](https://vedapath.app/en/mahabharata/udyoga-parva/188)). Ganga's curse is in Ganguli §189 and the Gita Press text 5.187. Whether it is in the critical text itself is **crit. ed. TBC**.

**What makes this a "Shraap" episode:** Amba's oath is a vow, not a curse (as `curses-catalog.md` warned). The curses that frame it are real and sourced:
1. **Ganga curses Amba**, and Ganga is **Bhishma's own mother** (§189).
2. **The Vasus, with Ganga's assent, curse Arjuna** for striking Bhishma from behind Shikhandin (Ashvamedhika §81). This is the natural tease for what comes after.

## Versions
- **Scripture (core text, critical edition):** the abduction, Shalva's rejection, Parashurama's 23-day duel, the tapas, Shiva's boon, the pyre, the rebirth as Shikhandin, the fall behind Shikhandin, "not Sikhandin's arrows".
- **Vulgate / to confirm in the critical edition:**
  - Ganga's curse (#11–12): in Ganguli and Gita Press.
  - The Vasus' curse (#22): Ashvamedhika, in Ganguli. Critical-edition status unconfirmed.
- **Popular retellings and TV (never present these as scripture):**
  - **"Amba begged Bhishma to marry her, and he refused."** In the text it is **Parashurama** who orders Bhishma to take her, and Bhishma refuses, citing his vow (Udyoga §181). Amba doesn't plead with him in person in the sections read.
  - **"Shikhandi was a eunuch / transgender."** The text: born a girl (Shikhandini), raised as a son by Shiva's word, then took manhood from a Yaksha (§191–195). Keep this respectful and stick to the text's own terms.
  - **"Amba got a garland of ever-fresh lotuses (from Kartikeya) that whoever wore it would kill Bhishma."** Found in many retellings; **not found in the Ganguli sections read (§174–195): UNSOURCED for now**. Don't use it.
  - TV serials often compress the 12 years of tapas and skip Ganga's curse entirely.

## Hook candidates
1. **"The woman who swore to kill Bhishma was cursed by Bhishma's own mother."** (Ganga, Udyoga §189)
2. **"Even Parashurama fought Bhishma for her: twenty-three days, and lost."** (§185, §188)
3. **"She waited a whole lifetime, and a rebirth, to keep one vow."** (§190, Bhishma §118–121)
4. **"Bhishma told his enemies exactly how to kill him."** (Bhishma §108)
5. **"'These arrows are not Shikhandi's.'** Bhishma knew who was really killing him." (§120)

## Sensitivity notes
- **Amba** is a wronged woman, not a villain or a "scorned lover": she was carried off, sent back, rejected by both men, and told she had "no other refuge". Show her with dignity and resolve.
- **Bhishma** is not a villain either: he lets her go, and refuses because of his vow. The tragedy is that everyone acts by their own dharma and she has nowhere left.
- **Shikhandi's gender:** use the text's terms (born a daughter, raised as a son, later a man). Never mock. In narration, prefer "reborn as Shikhandi" and keep the Yaksha exchange (#16) optional.
- **The pyre (#14):** show it symbolically (flames, a rising spark), never a body. Avoid any glorifying of self-harm: frame it as the vow carried into a new life, kept brief.
- **The war:** the arrows are symbolic. The bed of arrows is shown as Bhishma lying above the ground on arrow shafts, without gore.
