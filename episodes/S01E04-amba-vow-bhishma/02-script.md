# Script: S01E04 v2, The Woman Who Vowed to Kill Bhishma

**v2 (2026-11-19):** a short telling with one thread. v1 (23 beats, ~140 s) is kept in `review/02-script-v1.md`. The owner's note on v1: even at that length it wasn't clear; it had too many hiccups and shifts.

**What changed, and why:**
- **One spine, start to end:** *nowhere to go → her vow → his mother's curse → she doesn't stop → reborn → he falls.* Each beat leads into the next with "but / so / then / even".
- **The hook pays off inside the story.** v1 reached Ganga's curse at beat 12 of 23 and then kept going for 11 beats.
- **Detours cut:**
  - Parashurama's 23-day duel, a story of its own, now goes in the pinned comment.
  - Bhishma's vow is no longer a separate set-up beat; it's said once, where it matters (beat 5).
  - Half her body becoming the river.
  - Bhishma telling the Pandavas how to kill him.
  - The Vasus' curse on Arjuna, which was a second curse that competed with the first. It goes in the pinned comment or a later episode.
- **Characters on screen:** Amba, Bhishma, Shalva, Ganga, Shiva (as light), Shikhandi and Arjuna. v1 also had Parashurama, Drupada, Yudhishthira and the Vasus.

**Length:**
- **EN:** 179 words. At E04 v1's measured rate (2.54 words/s), that's **~71 s plus the 1 s sting**.
- **HI:** 203 words. At 2.62 words/s, that's **~78 s**.
- That's about half of v1 (140 / 151 s). The ready cuts below save ~5 s more in each language.

**Opening:** the series sting (**शाप IV / CURSED IV**).

**Accuracy:** every beat traces to the v1 research rows below. Ganga's curse (row 11) is Ganguli §189 and is in the critical edition (5.187.29–40, checked 2026-11-24).

## Accuracy notes
- **Beat 2:** "as a bride for his brother": Vichitravirya (Adi §102). The swayamvara and the abduction are row 1.
- **Beat 4:** shortens Shalva's "I won't take a bride another has won" (Udyoga §176).
- **Beat 5:** "sworn never to marry" is Bhishma's own reason, "my firm vow that I cannot abandon" (§181). The script doesn't say Amba begged him (a popular detail, not in the text).
- **Beat 6:** shortens "I will go… where I may myself slay Bhishma in battle" (§189).
- **Beat 7: KNOWN ERROR (found 2026-11-24, recorded and rendered as is).** "For twelve years she did tapas, standing in the Yamuna" / "बारह बरस… यमुना के जल में खड़े होकर" says she stood in the river for all twelve years. The text (5.187.19–22): six months on air, **one year** standing in the Yamuna, one year on her toes; twelve years in all. **Proposed:** EN *For twelve years she did tapas, one of them standing in the Yamuna.* HI *बारह बरस उन्होंने तप किया, एक बरस यमुना के जल में खड़े रहकर।*
- **Beat 8, place:** in the text Ganga meets her *after* the Yamuna, while she bathes at holy places in Vatsabhumi (5.187.23–29). The video keeps one riverbank; that's a visual compression, and the narration only says "rose from the water".
- **Beat 8:** shortens Ganga's curse (§189): if she dies under this vow, she will become a river crooked in her course, dry for eight months.
- **Beat 9:** Shiva's boon (§190), shortened.
- **Beat 10:** the pyre (§190), shown only as a flame motif with a rising spark. Rebirth as Shikhandi: rows 15–16, in the text's own terms.
- **Beat 11:** in the text Bhishma won't fight one who "was a female before" (Bhishma §108), and Arjuna attacks keeping Shikhandin before him (§118–120).
- **Beat 13:** the E05 tease follows the series plan: Karna's two curses (Parashurama's and the Brahmin's).

## Writing notes
- **Sensitivity:** unchanged from v1.
  - Amba is a wronged woman with nowhere left to go, never a "scorned lover".
  - Bhishma acts by his vow; he's not a villain.
  - Shikhandi is described in the text's terms and never mocked.
  - The pyre and the bed of arrows are symbolic, with no gore.
- **Storyboard:** every visual already exists from v1's assets. v2 needs fewer scenes (about 14), and no new assets.
