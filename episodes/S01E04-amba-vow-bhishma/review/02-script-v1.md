## Accuracy notes
- **Beat 2:** Bhishma's vow (research row 0, Adi §100) still needs a check against the text.
- **Beat 4:** in Adi §102 Ganguli prints "Salya" for the king Bhishma defeats. Udyoga §175–176 makes clear it is Shalva of Saubha, the one Amba loved.
- **Beat 7:** "Bhishma's vow meant he could never marry her" is exactly his answer to Parashurama: "my firm vow that I cannot abandon" (Udyoga §181). The script doesn't say Amba begged him in person (a popular detail, not in the text).
- **Beat 9:** the 23 days are in §185. "Parashurama conceded" is "I am vanquished, O Bhishma!" (§188), after the gods stopped Bhishma's Prasvapa weapon.
- **Beat 10:** Parashurama's words are in §189. Her reply shortens "I will go… where I may myself slay Bhishma in battle".
- **Beats 12–13:** Ganga's curse (§189): "acting crookedly"; a river "crooked in her course", "dry for eight months"; half her body became the river in Vatsabhumi.
- **Beat 14:** Shiva's words (§190), shortened: "Thou shalt slay him… and even obtain manhood… born in the race of Drupada".
- **Beat 15:** the pyre on the Yamuna's bank, "for Bhishma's destruction" (§190). It is shown only symbolically.
- **Beat 17:** Bhishma §108. In the text it's "one who was a female before"; "born a woman" / "जन्म से स्त्री" is a faithful shortening.
- **Beat 18:** "remembering his femininity… struck him not" (Bhishma §118). The script doesn't use the popular "lowered his bow".
- **Beat 19:** to Duhshasana, "with a smile": "These are not Sikhandin's" (§120).
- **Beat 22:** the Vasus, with Ganga's assent, curse Arjuna (Ashvamedhika §81). The script leaves out "to hell" and Ganga's part for time; both can go in the pinned comment.
- **Beat 23:** the E05 tease follows the series plan: Karna's curses (Parashurama's, and the Brahmin's over the cow).

## Writing notes
- **Sensitivity** (research → Sensitivity notes):
  - Amba is a wronged woman with nowhere left to go, never a "scorned lover".
  - Bhishma acts by his vow; he's not a villain.
  - Shikhandi is described in the text's terms and never mocked.
  - The pyre is a flame motif with a single rising spark.
  - The bed of arrows has no gore.
- **The two mothers:** Ganga curses Amba (beat 12) and, through the Vasus, assents to the curse on Arjuna (beat 22). It's a quiet symmetry. If time allows, the pinned comment can point it out.
