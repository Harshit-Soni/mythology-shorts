[slowly, gravely] And the man no one could defeat fell onto a bed of arrows.

[with awe, slowly] One vow, carried through death, had brought him down.

[ominous] But even this had a price: the Vasus cursed Arjuna… for striking from behind Shikhandi.

[ominous] And one warrior was already doomed by two curses before the war began: Karna.
