## Comment replies to have ready
- *"Amba begged Bhishma to marry her!"* → In the text it's **Parashurama** who orders Bhishma to take her, and Bhishma refuses: "my firm vow that I cannot abandon" (Udyoga §181).
- *"What about the garland of lotuses?"* → It's a popular detail, but we couldn't find it in the Mahabharata's Amba chapters (Udyoga §174–195), so we left it out.
- *"Was Shikhandi a eunuch / transgender?"* → The text says Shikhandi was born a daughter, raised as a son on Shiva's word, and later took manhood from a Yaksha named Sthunakarna (Udyoga §191–195). The video keeps to the text.
- *"Why didn't Bhishma fight Shikhandi?"* → He'd vowed never to fight one who "was a female before" (Bhishma Parva §108), and he knew who Shikhandi had been.
- *"Hiding behind Shikhandi was unfair!"* → The Vasus thought so too: they cursed Arjuna for it (Ashvamedhika §81). Years later, Ulupi arranged his release: Arjuna was killed by his own son Babhruvahana and brought back to life.
- *"Bhishma could choose when he died!"* → Yes: he lay on the bed of arrows until the sun turned north. That's a story for another day. 🙏
- *"Next: Karna?"* → Yes. Episode 5 is Karna, doomed by two curses before the war began. 🙏
