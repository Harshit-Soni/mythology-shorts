# S01E02: The Deer Who Cursed a King

- **Series:** S01 Shraap: Curses That Shaped the Mahabharata (episode 2)
- **Idea (one line):** King Pandu, hunting in the forest, shoots a mating deer that is really the sage Kindama with his wife. Dying, Kindama curses him to die the moment he lies with a woman. Pandu gives up the throne for the forest; his sons are born through Kunti's mantra from the gods; years later the curse comes true with Madri.
- **Why it will work:** One arrow decides the whole epic: without this curse there are no "sons of gods", no Pandavas as we know them, and no war. A sage hidden in a deer is a strong visual twist, and it pays off E01's tease (Vidura's brother Pandu). Cheap to make: Pandu, `forest-hunt` and `deer` are already in the library.
