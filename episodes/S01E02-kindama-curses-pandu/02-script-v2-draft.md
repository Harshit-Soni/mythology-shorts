# Script v2 (draft): S01E02, a slower, fuller telling

**Why:** v1 (13 beats, 145 words) felt rushed and skipped key steps. The biggest gap: it never said **why** Pandu needed sons. v2 adds those steps from the research table, gives each beat more room, and keeps the same opening and ending. Every new line traces to `01-research.md` (event numbers in the last column).

**Length:** EN ~200 words, about **85 s** with the sting and pauses. HI about the same. That's well past 60 s. It's fine for YouTube Shorts (up to 3 min) and Instagram Reels (90 s), but flag it. **Ready cuts** are at the bottom if you want it closer to 70 s.
**Cost to re-voice:** about 1,300 credits for EN and 1,250 for HI. The v1 takes used 958 and 861.

## Accuracy notes
- **Beat 9:** it names Durvasa as the giver of the mantra (§111). It still leaves out the vulgate's "foreseeing the curse" line, which isn't confirmed in the critical edition.
- **Beat 13:** "Madri tried to stop him" is the text (§125). It stays symbolic on screen, as in v1.
- **Beat 5:** Pandu's argument cites Agastya in the text. It's left out here for time.
