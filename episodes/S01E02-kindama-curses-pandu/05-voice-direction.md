### EN
```
[softly] The deer King Pandu shot… [shocked] cried out in a human voice.

Hunting in the Himalayas, Pandu shot a stag and its mate.

[with awe] But the stag was a sage, Kindama, living as a deer with his wife.

[gasping, pained] "Even sinful men don't kill at such a moment," he gasped.

[urgently] Pandu argued that kings may hunt deer. The sage was not moved.

[gravely, slowly] "Unite with your wife, and you will die. [pause] And she will follow you." [softly] Then the sage died.

[sadly] Pandu gave up his throne. His wives, Kunti and Madri, went with him to the mountains.

[gravely] But a man without sons could not enter heaven.

[warmly] Then Kunti told him her secret: a mantra from the sage Durvasa, to call any god.

Pandu chose the first: Dharma, god of justice. [tenderly] Yudhishthira was born.

[building wonder] Then Vayu gave Bhima, who fell from Kunti's lap as a baby and shattered a rock.

[building wonder] Indra gave Arjuna. And Kunti shared the mantra with Madri, who called the Ashvins: twins, Nakula and Sahadeva.

[softly, slowly] Years later, one spring, Pandu forgot the curse. Madri tried to stop him… [pause] but it took him.

[hushed] As the curse foretold, Madri followed him into the fire, asking Kunti to raise her sons.

[with awe, slowly] One arrow… and five sons of gods.

[ominous] And one of them, Arjuna, would face a curse of his own.
```

[softly, slowly] बरसों बाद, एक बसंत में, पाण्डु शाप भूल गए। माद्री ने रोका… [pause] पर शाप सच हो गया।

[hushed] शाप के अनुसार, माद्री भी उनके साथ चिता पर चढ़ गईं, कुन्ती से अपने बेटों को सँभालने को कहकर।

[with awe, slowly] एक बाण… और पाँच देवपुत्र।

[ominous] और उनमें से एक, अर्जुन, पर भी शाप पड़ना अभी बाकी था।
