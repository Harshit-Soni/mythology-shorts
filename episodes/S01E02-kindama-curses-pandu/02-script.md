## Accuracy notes (v2 additions)
- **Beat 9:** it names Durvasa as the giver of the mantra (§111). It still leaves out the vulgate's "foreseeing the curse" line, which isn't confirmed in the critical edition.
- **Beat 13:** "Madri tried to stop him" is the text (§125). It stays symbolic on screen, as in v1.
- **Beat 5:** Pandu's argument cites Agastya in the text. It's left out here for time.

## Writing notes
- **Sensitivity** (see research → Sensitivity notes): the mating deer are shown only as two deer close together; Pandu's death is symbolic (blossoms, Madri turning away); the pyre is a stylised flame motif. The script never says Madri tempted him: the text says she resisted.
- **Beat 9** names Durvasa as the giver of the mantra (§111). The vulgate line that he gave it *foreseeing the curse* stays out until it's confirmed in the critical edition.
- **Beat 10** is the E01 callback: Pandu's first chosen god is **Dharma**, the same god Mandavya cursed. A small on-screen tag carries it without extra narration.
- **Assets:** reuse Pandu, Dharma, `forest-hunt`, `deer`, `shaap-stamp`. New: Kindama (derive from Mandavya), Kunti and Madri (derive from existing rigs), babies, a spring-blossom forest and a mountain background. The gods in beat 9 are symbols (wind, vajra, twin stars), so no new god rigs.
- Mandavya's curse used तुम; Kindama's does too (मरोगे), for force.
