## Writing notes
- **Sensitivity** (see research → Sensitivity notes): the mating deer are shown only as two deer close together; Pandu's death is symbolic (blossoms, Madri turning away); the pyre is a stylised flame motif. The script never says Madri tempted him: the text says she resisted.
- **Beat 7** says only "a sage's mantra". The vulgate line that Durvasa gave it *foreseeing the curse* (§111) stays out until it's confirmed in the critical edition.
- **Beat 8** is the E01 callback: Pandu's first chosen god is **Dharma**, the same god Mandavya cursed. A small on-screen tag carries it without extra narration.
- **Assets:** reuse Pandu, Dharma, `forest-hunt`, `deer`, `shaap-stamp`. New: Kindama (derive from Mandavya), Kunti and Madri (derive from existing rigs), babies, a spring-blossom forest and a mountain background. The gods in beat 9 are symbols (wind, vajra, twin stars), so no new god rigs.
- Mandavya's curse used तुम; Kindama's does too (मरोगे), for force.
