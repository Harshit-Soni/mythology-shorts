## Comment replies to have ready
- *"They were tigers, not deer!"* → That's from a TV version. The Mahabharata says Kindama and his wife took the form of **deer** (Adi Parva §118).
- *"Madri tempted him / Pandu died of love"* → The text says the opposite: **Madri tried to stop him**, and he forgot the curse (Adi Parva §125). That's why the video shows her turning away.
- *"Why didn't Pandu get the sin of killing a Brahmin?"* → Kindama said so himself: Pandu didn't know he was a sage, so the sin of brahmahatya wouldn't be his. The curse was for killing at that moment (Adi Parva §118).
- *"Kings are allowed to hunt!"* → Pandu made exactly that argument and cited the sage Agastya. Kindama agreed that hunting wasn't the wrong; striking at that moment was (§118).
- *"What about Karna?"* → Yes: Kunti got the mantra from the sage Durvasa as a girl and tested it on Surya, and Karna was born, years before (Adi Parva §111). His curses are coming later in the series.
- *"Why did Madri go and not Kunti?"* → Both wanted to. Madri said that he had died with her, and the curse said that the wife with him would follow. She asked Kunti to raise her twins (§125).
- *"Next: Urvashi and Arjuna?"* → Yes, Episode 3. 🙏
