## Next-episode tease
Beat 16 leads into **S01E02: Kindama curses Pandu**. Pandu is Vidura's half-brother (both are Vyasa's sons, Adi §106), so the link is accurate.

## Kept out of the video (use in the pinned comment / description)
- **The ending:** at the end of his life, Vidura merged into Yudhishthira, Dharma's own son (Ashramavasika Parva §26). Good pinned comment: *"Did you know how Vidura's life ended? He didn't die like anyone else…"*
- The god is **Dharma**. Many people know him as Yama (Dharmaraja). Expect comments about this and reply with the source.

## Writing notes
- Dharma speaks to the sage respectfully (आपने), as in the text ("O thou of ascetic wealth"). Mandavya's curse uses तुम for force.
- "Servant woman's son" / दासी के पुत्र is the respectful way to state the "Shudra woman" in the epic for a general audience. The research file has the exact wording.
- No gore: the impalement is shown only as a silhouette and a symbol.
