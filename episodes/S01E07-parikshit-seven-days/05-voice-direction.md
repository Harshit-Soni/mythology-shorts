```
[matter-of-fact] In the Mahabharata, King Parikshit lost a deer he was hunting.

[sad, gentle] "He didn't know of my vow," said his father. [gravely] But a curse can't be unsaid.

[hushed] Then Takshaka hid as a tiny worm in a fruit, sent as a gift.

[slowly] The last sun was setting. [laughing] "I'm safe," laughed the king. "Let this worm be Takshaka, and the curse come true!"

[hushed, slowly] He put it on his neck. [ominous] It was Takshaka.
```

```
[hushed, slowly] और उसे गर्दन पर रख लिया। [ominous] वो सच में तक्षक था।

[reflective, slowly] एक मरा साँप, ग़ुस्से में उठाया। एक ज़िंदा, हँसी में।

[intrigued] अगली बार: वो माँ, जिसने कृष्ण को शाप दिया।
```
