## Accuracy notes
- **The curse is from the vulgate only** (research → warning). Beat 6 says so once, *"As the popular telling goes" / "प्रचलित कथा के अनुसार"*, before the night visit, and a small on-screen tag carries it. Beats 6–11 sit under that frame. Everything else is in the critical edition and is told plainly: Amaravati, the weapons, Chitrasena's dance lessons, Urvashi at the dance, Brihannala, the cattle raid.
- **Beat 4:** Indra telling Arjuna to learn dance, *"it will be to thy benefit"* (Vana §44), **is** in the critical edition. That's why the twist holds up even without the curse.
- **Beat 6:** "sent by Indra" is kept on purpose. In the text she came at Indra's command (§45–46), which is fair to Urvashi.
- **Beat 8:** Pururavas is an *ancestor* of the Kuru line (Adi §75), not "the first king". The script says only "ancestor".
- **Beat 10:** her words, shortened: *"among women… as a dancer… destitute of manhood"* (§46). The fourth phrase, "scorned as a eunuch", is left out (sensitivity).
- **Beat 11:** Indra's words, shortened: the curse will be to his benefit, in the 13th year of hiding, for one year (§46). "The year you must hide" = the year of *ajñātavāsa*.
- **Beat 14:** the stunned warriors are Drona, Kripa, Karna and the rest. **Bhishma was not stunned** (Virata §65), so the script leaves him out of the list. That also sets up beat 16.
- **Beat 16 (E04 tease):** Amba's vow against Bhishma, which leads to Shikhandi.

## Writing notes
- **Sensitivity:**
  - Urvashi is never described by her body. She is shown as a proud, wronged figure, and she came at Indra's word.
  - Brihannala is shown with dignity: gifted, then heroic.
  - Beat 15 says the curse was *meant as punishment*; it doesn't call Brihannala shameful.
  - The Hindi uses *नर्तक* / *नृत्य-शिक्षिका*, never *हिजड़ा*.
- **E02 link:** beat 1 pays off E02's tease ("Arjuna would face a curse of his own") straight away.
- **Assets (for the storyboard):**
  - **Reuse:** Arjuna, `amaravati-court` (built in E02 for this episode), Kunti, Indra's vajra (`vajra-flash` from E02), `shaap-stamp`, `forest-hunt`.
  - **New:** Urvashi (derive from Kunti/Madri), Indra (derive from a king rig + halo), Chitrasena (derive), Brihannala (Arjuna with a braid, earrings, bangles), Virata, Uttara, a shami tree prop, the Gandiva, Virata's court background, a cattle-raid army in silhouette.
- Urvashi's curse uses the तुम form (रहोगे), like Mandavya's and Kindama's, for force.
