# Script: S01E03, The Apsara Who Cursed Arjuna (v2)

**Why v2:** the owner's feedback on v1 (2026-11-17): the second half moved too fast and left things hanging. It went from "the Kauravas raided the cattle" straight to "took the Gandiva", which was confusing. v1 is kept in `review/02-script-v1.md`. This version slows down from the curse onward:
- **The stakes:** why hiding mattered (beat 13).
- **How the dance teacher ends up on a battlefield:** beats 16–18.
- **The reveal "I am Arjuna":** beat 19.
- **The year that ran out for the Kauravas:** beat 21.
- **The Uttara mirror:** beat 22.
- **Fairness to Urvashi:** beat 10.

Beats 1–9 are unchanged from v1 (same words, so the same scenes).

**Opening:** the series sting (≈1 s: **शाप III / CURSED III**), visual only.
**Accuracy frame:** Urvashi's visit and curse (beats 6–12) are the popular (vulgate) text. Beat 6 says so once. Everything from beat 13 on is in the core text.

## Accuracy notes
- **Beat 10:** Urvashi's argument, shortened: apsaras are "free and unconfined in our choice" (Vana §46, vulgate).
- **Beat 13:** the terms of the dice game: 12 years in the forest and the 13th year unrecognised; if recognised, another 12 years of exile (Sabha §75). The Hindi "फिर से वही सब" stays loose on purpose.
- **Beat 16:** Duryodhana knew the year was mostly over and was looking for the Pandavas (Virata §26). The Trigartas drew King Virata away, and the Kauravas seized sixty thousand cows (§30, §35).
- **Beat 17:**
  - Uttar boasts that he only needs a charioteer (§36).
  - The Sairindhri (Draupadi in disguise) names Brihannala, and Princess Uttara asks Brihannala to drive (§37).
  - The script says "his sister begged her dance teacher", which is true. It leaves out the Sairindhri for time.
- **Beat 18:** Uttar jumps down and runs; Arjuna runs after him, "his own long braid and pure red garments fluttering in the air"; soldiers laugh, and he catches Uttar within a hundred steps (§38).
- **Beat 19:** the weapons were hidden in the shami tree (§5, §38–43). "I am Arjuna, called also Partha" (§44); he then lists his ten names.
- **Beat 20:** Drona, Kripa, Karna and the rest are stunned; **Bhishma is not** (§65). Bhishma is left out of this list.
- **Beat 21:** Bhishma's calculation, with the extra months, shows the thirteen years had passed (§52). So the reveal came after the year had ended.
- **Beat 22:** Virata §72. Arjuna also says he wants to protect Uttara's good name; the script keeps only "she had trusted him like a father".

## Writing notes
- **Pacing:**
  - Beats 16–19 each answer one question, in order: why there's a battle, who fights, how the dance teacher gets there, and how the disguise ends.
  - The reveal line "I am Arjuna" gets its own pause (the "…").
- **Tone:** Uttar's flight is comic in the text. Keep it light, but never mock Brihannala: the soldiers laugh at the sight, and the reveal turns that laugh around.
- **New assets this needs** (all in code, from existing rigs):
  - **Prince Uttar:** derived from Arjuna or Pandu, younger.
  - **A war chariot:** derived from `celestial-chariot`, earthly.
  - **A dice board:** for beat 13.
  - **Cattle in dust:** silhouettes.
  - **New poses:** Brihannala running, Uttar fleeing, Urvashi arguing, Arjuna blessing.
  - **Young Abhimanyu:** for beat 22.
