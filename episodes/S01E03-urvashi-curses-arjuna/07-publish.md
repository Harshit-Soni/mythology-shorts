## Comment replies to have ready
- *"This isn't in the real Mahabharata!"* → You're partly right. The Urvashi episode is in the popular text (Ganguli's translation follows it) but not in the BORI critical edition, which is why the video says "as the popular telling goes". The dance lessons in heaven and Brihannala **are** in the critical edition.
- *"Urvashi was his dance teacher!"* → A common mix-up. The text says the Gandharva **Chitrasena** taught him (Vana §44). Urvashi was one of the apsaras who danced at Indra's court.
- *"Why did he call her mother?"* → Urvashi was the wife of King Pururavas. Their son Ayus began the line that leads to the Kurus, so to Arjuna she was an ancestress, "the mother of the Kaurava race" (Vana §46, Adi §75).
- *"Urvashi was the villain."* → The text is fairer to her. She came because Indra sent her, and she argued that apsaras are free to choose. Arjuna held to his own rule. That's the debate.
- *"Arjuna became a woman?"* → The text's word is *klība*, neither man nor woman. He dressed as a woman (braid, earrings, bangles) and called himself Brihannala (Virata §2).
- *"Who was Uttar's charioteer meant to be?"* → Uttar had no charioteer; the Sairindhri (Draupadi in disguise) said Brihannala had once driven for Arjuna, and Princess Uttara asked her teacher (Virata §36–37).
- *"Why wasn't Bhishma stunned?"* → The text says Bhishma knew how to counter the weapon (Virata §65). He's at the centre of the next episode.
- *"Next: Shikhandi?"* → Yes. Episode 4 is Amba's vow against Bhishma. 🙏
