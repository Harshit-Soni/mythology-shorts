# Script: S01E08, The Mother Who Cursed Krishna (season finale)

**Spine:** *"You could have stopped this. Why did you look on?"* → her curse: the same end for your clan → he smiles; he knows → thirty-six years later he could stop it, and lets it come → the one who wounds him is comforted.
**Opening:** the series sting (**शाप VIII / CURSED VIII**, ≈1 s).
**Length:** EN 200 words ≈ 78 s, HI 211 words ≈ 82 s, both with the sting, at E07's measured rates (2.6 / 2.58 words/s). A little over the 75 s aim, for the finale.
**Source used:** the critical edition, Stri Parva 11.13–15, 11.25 and Mausala Parva 16.3–5; Ganguli agrees. **The research rows are not yet verified by the owner.** The Puranas' fish and arrowhead, Vali, and the iron body stay out.
**Source on screen:** "Mahabharata, Stri Parva" / "महाभारत, स्त्री पर्व" (beat 4); "Mausala Parva" / "मौसल पर्व" (beat 8).

## Accuracy notes (each shortened line against its row)
- **Beat 1:** smiled: *īṣad abhyutsmayann iva* (11.25.43). "Made it come true": *cikīrṣan satyam eva tat* (16.3.21).
- **Beat 2:** "all hundred": Duryodhana and his brothers (1.107.37; 11.14.20). Her daughter Duhshala lives.
- **Beat 3:** **"held back"**: she wished to curse (11.13.2); Vyasa stopped her; then she names Duryodhana, Shakuni, Karna and Duhshasana (11.13.14). "My own son caused this" shortens that. She comforts them "like a mother" (11.15.8): in the picture. HI *"शाप नहीं दिया"* is true.
- **Beat 4:** she sees the field by divine sight, then goes there (11.16.1–4, 9–11): **she stays blindfolded**. "Why did you look on?": *upekṣitāḥ* (11.25.36).
- **Beat 5:** the peace mission (5.91, 5.93); Duryodhana refused. Gandhari pleaded with him too (5.127): for the replies.
- **Beat 6:** 11.25.38–41. "You let my clan die" = you looked on while Kurus and Pandavas killed each other. "You'll destroy yours" = *jñātīn vadhiṣyasi*. "Meet your end wandering the forest" = *vanecaraḥ… nidhanaṃ samavāpsyasi*. Left out: "by a wretched means"; her tapas as the power; his women's grief.
- **Beat 7:** 11.25.43–44. "They'll fall by one another's hands" (11.25.45) is cut; beat 10 shows it. **His stern words after this (11.26.1–5) are left out;** they're in the replies. Nothing here says he comforted or thanked her.
- **Beat 8:** 16.3.16–20. **The text says he remembered Gandhari, but the sages' curse of the pestle (16.2) is the epic's main cause.** We say "her words", never "because of her curse". The reply must have the pestle.
- **Beat 9:** "could have stopped it": Vyasa, *śaktenāpi vyapohitum* (16.9.26–27), said of the sages' curse. He ordered the pilgrimage (16.3.21–22); he was with them at Prabhasa (16.4.15), so "led".
- **Beat 10:** "old quarrel from the war": Satyaki and Kritavarma (16.4.16–27). "Looked on, knowing the hour": *jānan kālasya paryayam… tasthau* (16.4.42). **He isn't only a watcher:** he first ran to stop it (16.4.28), and struck when his son fell (16.4.34–35, 44). Not in the narration; the reply must have it. HI "सब आपस में लड़ मरे": by one another, 16.4.36–41.
- **Beat 11:** 16.5.16–20. The arrow struck the sole; only the flight is shown.
- **Beat 12:** *āśvāsayat* (comforted), *rodasī vyāpya lakṣmyā* (filling heaven and earth with splendour), 16.5.21. **"Didn't curse him"** is the narrator's contrast; true, and the text reports no curse.
- **Beat 13:** the narrator's close. "Eight stories" = the eight episodes. "Mercy" / "करुणा" reads beat 12.
- **Order in time:** E07's Parikshit lives after Krishna. Nothing here says otherwise.

## Sensitivity
- **Krishna** is calm, knowing and unhurt in every picture: no wound, no blood, no body. "Lay down", "rose"; never "was killed", "died", "punished".
- **Gandhari** is truthful and broken by grief, never bitter.
- **The field and Prabhasa** are lamps, wheels, silhouettes, grass. No bodies, no drinking.
- **The hunter** is an ordinary, horrified man.
