**Accuracy:**
- The curse, Krishna's answer, the thirty-six years, the clan's end, and the hunter are all in the critical edition (11.25, 16.3–5), and Ganguli (Stri §25, Mausala §2–4) matches.
- **Two curses end the Yadavas.** The epic's main cause is the sages' curse of the iron pestle (16.2); Gandhari's words are what Krishna remembers (16.3.19, 16.5.17). Say "her words came true", never "her curse alone destroyed them". The pestle goes in the replies.
- **Krishna is never "killed", "punished" or "defeated"** in the copy. He knew, accepted, lay down in yoga and rose in light (16.5.18–25).
- **He wasn't only a watcher at the sea:** he first ran to stop it, and struck when his son fell (16.4.28, 34–35). The replies say so.
- **Keep out:** Jara as Vali reborn, the blindfold removed to make Duryodhana's body like iron, and the fish that swallowed the pestle's last piece (the Vishnu Purana's). None is in the epic passages read.
- **Ashwatthama: three thousand years** (CE 10.16.10), never "forever".

## Comment replies to have ready
- *"Krishna was cursed by the sages too, the iron pestle!"* → Yes. The epic names two causes: the sages' curse on Samba (the iron pestle) and Gandhari's words, which Krishna himself remembers (Mausala §1–2, §4). We followed Gandhari's thread.
- *"Krishna just stood and watched his clan die?"* → Not only. He first ran to stop the fight, and when his own son fell, he struck too. Then, knowing the hour had come, he stood still (Mausala §3).
- *"Why didn't Krishna stop the war?"* → He tried: before it, he went to the Kaurava court as an envoy for peace, and Duryodhana refused (Udyoga Parva). Gandhari herself had begged her son to make peace (Udyoga Parva).
- *"What did Krishna say after the curse?"* → He smiled and said only he could end his clan. Then, sternly: the Kurus died by your own fault, for you favoured Duryodhana (Stri §25–26).
- *"Jara was Vali reborn!"* → That's a later tradition. In the Mahabharata passages we used, Jara is simply a hunter, and Krishna comforts him (Mausala §4).
- *"The arrow was made from the pestle, swallowed by a fish."* → That's in the Vishnu and Bhagavata Puranas. The Mahabharata's telling doesn't have it.
- *"Gandhari opened her blindfold to make Duryodhana's body iron."* → We didn't find that in the epic. There, Danavas tell Duryodhana that Shiva made his upper body hard as diamond (Vana Parva, critical edition 3.240).
- *"Why the sole of the foot?"* → At the end Krishna recalls the sage Durvasa's words, spoken when Krishna smeared himself with the sage's leftover payasa (Mausala §4). The text here doesn't spell out more.
- *"Krishna died?"* → The text says he lay down in yoga, comforted the hunter, and rose, filling earth and sky with light, to his own place (Mausala §4).
- *"Didn't Gandhari curse the Pandavas?"* → She wanted to; Vyasa reminded her of her own words, "where dharma is, there is victory". Her glance from under the blindfold only marked Yudhishthira's toenail, and she comforted them like a mother (Stri §14–15).
- *"Ashwatthama is cursed forever."* → Krishna's words in the epic: three thousand years of wandering, alone (Sauptika §16). That's the story for Season 2. 🙏
- *"Is this the end?"* → Of Season 1, yes. Season 2 begins with Ashwatthama. 🙏
