# Script: S01E06, The King of Heaven Who Became a Snake

**Spine:** a humble king → power → pride → a trap he walks into → the curse → his own descendant frees him with an answer. Each beat follows from the last. Beat 4's power returns as the snake's grip in beat 10; beat 3's "too weak" returns in beat 14.
**Opening:** the series sting (**शाप VI / CURSED VI**, ≈1 s).
**Length:** EN 170 words ≈ 68 s, HI 176 words ≈ 74 s, both including the 1 s sting (at E05's measured rates).
**Source used:** Udyoga Parva (CE 5.11–17) for the rise and the curse; Vana Parva (CE 3.175–178) for the way out and the release. **The research rows are not yet verified by the owner.**
**Source on screen:** "Mahabharata, Udyoga & Vana Parva" / "महाभारत, उद्योग और वन पर्व".

## Accuracy notes (each shortened line against its row)
- **Beat 1:** he is called a human king (*mānuṣo rājā*, 5.16.21). "He became a snake" is the curse, not the end of the story; beat 14 completes it.
- **Beat 2:** the three nouns are in order: the throne (beats 3–5), the palanquin (6–7), the riddle (11). "Riddle" = the snake's questions.
- **Beat 3:** why Indra vanished (guilt after Vritra) is left out. "I'm too weak": *durbalo 'ham* (5.11.3).
- **Beat 4:** 5.11.6, from the gods and sages.
- **Beat 5:** "demanded": he orders that she come to him (5.11.14–15). "Unstoppable" / "कौन रोकता" is the narrator's summary; the gods do plead with him, and Brihaspati shelters her.
- **Beat 6:** it is **Indra's** plan, spoken by Shachi (5.15.1–13). The script credits her with setting the trap, which she does; the picture shows Indra behind it. "No god ever has" shortens "a vehicle not Vishnu's, nor Rudra's". The Hindi says "ऐसे कोई देवता नहीं आया".
- **Beat 7:** **"set his foot on", never "kicked"** (5.17.11). The quarrel over the Vedic hymns, one of Agastya's three reasons, is left out.
- **Beat 8:** **"ten thousand years" is left out** so it doesn't clash with beat 9 (research, trap 1).
- **Beat 9:** from the snake's own account (3.178.39–40). "Took pity" = *kripanvitah*; Nahusha's plea for mercy, which comes first, is only in the picture. **This is where the script moves from account A to account B**; both have Agastya cursing.
- **Beat 10:** "Ages later" / "बहुत समय बाद": no figure given. The snake's boon: 3.176.22. "अजगर" is the text's own word (3.175.1); English says "giant snake".
- **Beat 11:** 3.176.13–14 and 3.177.12, 15. It asks more than one thing; only the first question is used.
- **Beat 12:** three of the seven qualities in 3.177.16 (truth, giving, forgiveness, good conduct, kindness, self-control, compassion). "Not birth alone" follows 3.177.20–21 and Ganguli's "not… by birth alone".
- **Beat 13:** 3.178.29–31. The verse says *riddhi* (prosperity) and then *aishvarya* (lordship): English "power", Hindi "वैभव". Yudhishthira's question ("how did you fall?") and the later questions are left out; in the text the curse ends after the whole talk.
- **Beat 14:** 3.178.32, 44–45. The curse ends through talking with a good man. Bhima's release is in the picture.
- **Beat 15:** checked 2026-11-25: a king (Parikshit) lays a dead snake on a sage's shoulder and is cursed to die within seven days ([CE 1.36.18, 1.37.11–19](https://bombay.indology.info/mahabharata/text/UR/MBh01.txt)).
- **Left out on purpose:** "Sarpa, sarpa!" (Devi Bhagavata, not the epic passages read) and Bhrigu (account C). Both suit the pinned comment.

## Sensitivity
- Shachi is calm and clever; nothing suggestive. "Demanded" / "माँग बैठे" is as far as it goes.
- Beat 12 is Yudhishthira's own answer, close to the text, with no comment added.
- No bite, no blood: coils and light only.
