## Comment replies to have ready
- *"It was the Earth's curse, for squeezing ghee out of the soil!"* → That's a popular later story. In the Mahabharata chapters we used, the wheel curse comes from the Brahmin whose cow Karna killed by accident (Shanti §2).
- *"Karna forgot the Brahmastra!"* → Almost: the weapon Parashurama taught would fail him at the hour of his death, against his equal (Shanti §3). At that moment it doesn't come to him, though the text has him invoke a Brahmastra again soon after (Karna §90).
- *"The insect was Indra!"* → Both are in the epic. Karna himself says it was Indra, helping Arjuna (Karna §42). Narada says it was Alarka, an Asura under a curse (Shanti §3).
- *"It was a bee / a scorpion."* → That's from retellings. The Sanskrit says a worm or insect (*krimi*, *kita*).
- *"It was a calf, not a cow."* → Narada says the cow (Shanti §2); Karna's own account says her calf (Karna §42). We followed Narada.
- *"Drona refused because of caste!"* → The text gives Drona's reasons: partiality for Arjuna, and distrust of Karna. His stated rule is that only a vow-keeping Brahmin or an ascetic Kshatriya may learn it (Shanti §2).
- *"Arjuna killed him while he was unarmed!"* → Karna had asked for a moment to free the wheel. He fought on after Krishna's answer, and was killed while the wheel still held him (Karna §90–91).
- *"What about his armour and earrings?"* → He'd already given them to Indra (Shanti §5). That's a story of its own.
- *"Next: a king who became a snake?"* → Yes. Episode 6 is Nahusha, who ruled heaven until a sage's curse brought him down. 🙏
