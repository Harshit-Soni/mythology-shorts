# Research: S01E05

**Read on 2026-11-24:**
- **Vulgate:** K. M. Ganguli's public-domain translation on sacred-texts.com: Shanti §II–V, Karna §42, §90, §91.
- **Critical edition (CE):** the BORI Sanskrit text at [bombay.indology.info](https://bombay.indology.info/mahabharata/statement.html): [MBh 12](https://bombay.indology.info/mahabharata/text/UR/MBh12.txt) (12.1–3, 12.5) and [MBh 8](https://bombay.indology.info/mahabharata/text/UR/MBh08.txt) (8.29, 8.65–67). The English of CE verses below is Claude's own working translation.

**Headline:** both curses, the cow, the insect and the wheel are in the **critical edition**. Both curses fall during one stay with Parashurama, so they belong to one quest. In the last battle, **one verse names both** (CE 8.66.42).

**Two accounts inside the epic.** Both are core text, and they differ:
- **Narada's account**, told to Yudhishthira after the war (Shanti, CE 12.2–3).
- **Karna's own account**, told to Shalya before his last battle (Karna Parva, CE 8.29).

Pick one for the script. **Recommended: Narada's**, because it gives both scenes in order.

## Traps in the text (read before scripting)
1. **"He forgot the Brahmastra" is not the whole story.** The verse says the weapon learned from Rama did not appear (8.66.42). But a few verses later Karna **does** invoke the Brahmastra (CE 8.66.48, again 8.67.8; Ganguli too), and Arjuna destroys it. **Safe wording:** "the weapon would not come to him". **Avoid:** "he never used it again", "he forgot it forever".
2. **The terms of curse 2** (CE): the weapon works for him *except* at the time of his death against an equal. So it isn't lost until then.
3. **What was killed:** the homa **cow** in Narada's account (12.2.20); her **calf** in Karna's own (8.29.33). Both are core text. With Narada's account, say "cow".
4. **Who the insect was:** the cursed Asura Alarka in Narada's account; **Indra**, helping Arjuna, in Karna's own (8.29.4–5, also CE). "Indra as the insect" is therefore **not** just TV. Don't state either as the only version: the script can simply say "an insect", and the pinned comment can give both.
5. **Order:** Narada tells the cow first, then the insect. Karna's own account mentions Parashurama's curse first and gives no clear order.
6. **Only in the vulgate (not in the CE verses read):** Kala's warning "the Earth is devouring thy wheel"; the **left** wheel; the earth rising four fingers as he pulls; "eleven bowstrings"; Arjuna stunned and dropping the Gandiva; Krishna's longer list (the lac house, Abhimanyu).
7. **He's not shot "in the back" or "unarmed".** In both texts he fights on after Krishna's answer, and tries again to free the chariot. Say he was killed "while the wheel held him".
8. **Intent:** the cow was killed **unknowingly**, and Karna confessed at once. Don't show him as cruel. The lie to Parashurama was knowing.

## Versions
- **Scripture (critical edition):** Drona's refusal, the lie, the cow (or calf), the insect (Alarka, or Indra), both curses, the wheel, the weapon failing to appear, Krishna's answer, the Anjalika arrow.
- **Vulgate only:** trap 6 above.
- **Later or popular retellings (never present these as scripture):**
  - **Bhudevi (Earth) curses Karna** for squeezing spilled ghee or milk out of the soil for a crying girl. It's in modern retellings ([Scroll](https://scroll.in/article/1076892/mythology-for-children-the-curse-that-proved-fatal-for-karna-in-the-battle-of-kurukshetra), [Devdutt Pattanaik](https://devdutt.com/?p=25594)); it isn't in any of the sections read, and its origin is **UNSOURCED**. Leave it out, or mention it only in the pinned comment as "in some tellings".
  - **The insect as a bee or a scorpion:** popular retellings ([bee](https://www.hindu-blog.com/2026/07/story-indra-taking-form-of-bee-to.html)). The text says *kṛmi* (worm) and *kīṭa* (insect).
  - **"Karna forgot the mantra":** the usual shorthand; see trap 1.

## Hook candidates
1. **"Everyone knows Karna's wheel sank. Almost no one asks why."** The wheel is what viewers already know; the cow's curse is its hidden origin (12.2, 8.66.42). **Recommended**, followed by the trailer line "a lie, a cow, and an insect".
2. **"After the war, Yudhishthira asked one question: why did the earth swallow my brother's wheel?"** (12.1.43). True to the text's own frame, but "brother" needs explaining.
3. **"Karna was caught because he didn't cry out."** (12.3.8–11) The irony at the centre.
4. **"Two curses. One weapon, one wheel. And they came due in the same moment."** (8.66.42)
5. **"Karna knew how he would die, and rode out anyway."** (8.29)

## Closing the loop
- **The Brahmin's words pay off exactly:** killed *heedless*, as the cow was (12.2.26 → 8.67.23, caught up with the wheel). Plant "unaware" early.
- **End on the wheel**, then the light rising from him (8.67.27), then the tease for E06 (Nahusha).
- E04's tease promised "cursed twice before the war began": both curses are indeed long before the war.

## Sensitivity notes
- **Karna's birth:** the text has him call himself a *Suta*. Use "a charioteer's son" / "सूतपुत्र" neutrally; never as an insult, and don't draw a caste moral. Drona's rule and Parashurama's remark are the characters' words, so attribute them.
- **The cow:** its death is off-screen (an arrow's flight, then a bell on the ground). No wound, no blood.
- **The insect:** a thin red line at most. Show the strain on his face rather than the wound.
- **Karna's death:** symbolic only (the setting sun, the light rising). No severed head.
- **Krishna's answer** refers to Draupadi's humiliation: one short clause, nothing shown.
