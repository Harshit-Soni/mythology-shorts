## Rules
- **Dignity (CLAUDE.md rule 2):** Hanuman is a deity and a devotee. He's strong and calm, never a cartoon monkey: no grin, no comic snout, no bananas. Palms joined, his mace beside him.
- **Iconography (traditional, design choices noted):**
  - **Hanuman:** a vanara face, meaning a full rounded muzzle (the *hanu*, jaw, of his name) on a man's strong body; a long tail that curls up behind; a gold crown; earrings; a gold mace (*gada*). The colours are a design choice.
  - **Rama:** dark skin (*śyāma* in Valmiki), a crown, a bow and quiver, yellow silk, a faint halo. He must **not** read as Krishna: no peacock feather or flute, and a different skin tone.
- **References:** only openly licensed ones, if any are added later (Wikimedia Commons, museum open access). No other channels' art.

## Variations
| | Hanuman | Rama |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Saffron:** sindoor-orange body, pale muzzle, red dhoti, gold sash. The temple-image Hanuman most viewers know. | **Durva green:** the dark green of *durva* grass, a traditional way of painting Rama's *śyāma* skin. Clearly not Krishna. |
| **B** | **Golden fur:** a tawny-gold body, cream muzzle, saffron dhoti, red sash | **Deep indigo:** very close to Krishna's blue |
| **C** | **Earth-brown:** a brown body, tan muzzle, yellow dhoti, red sash | **Blue-grey:** dusky, a bit drab at phone size |

## Recommendation (Claude's pick, waiting for the owner)
- **Hanuman A (saffron).** It's the most recognisable at phone size and in a thumbnail, and it's the colour of his temple images. It also stands apart from both Rama and the sky backgrounds. B is a close second.
- **Rama A (durva green).** It's the only one that can't be mistaken for Krishna side by side. B and C fall into Krishna's blue.
