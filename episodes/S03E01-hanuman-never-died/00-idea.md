# S03E01: The One Who Asked to Live as Long as a Story

- **Series:** S03 Chiranjeevi: the ones who never died (episode 1, **series opener**)
- **Idea (one line):** Hanuman is one of the seven "who never die". Why? When Rama was leaving the world, he asked his devoted friend what he wanted. Hanuman asked for one thing: to live on earth for as long as Rama's story is told. Rama granted it, so he is still here, wherever the story is told.
- **Why it will work:**
  - Everyone knows Hanuman; almost no one knows *why* he is called immortal, or that he chose it himself.
  - The answer is a turn, not a power: he lives on for a story, not for his own sake. A warm, devotional ending that viewers share.
  - It sets the series question cleanly (*why can't he die?*), and the payoff ("as long as this story is told... you're telling it right now") lands on the viewer.
- **Created:** 2026-11-26

## Angle (from memory; every point to be sourced in research)
- **One spine:** the question (is Hanuman still alive?) → Rama, at the end of his reign, asks his companions to follow him or stay → Hanuman's request → Rama's boon → so he lives on, wherever the Ramayana is told.
- **Hook options:**
  1. *Hanuman never died. Not because of a weapon, or a curse. He asked for it, and he gave a reason.*
  2. *Rama offered Hanuman anything. He asked to stay behind.*
  3. *Hanuman can't die until one thing ends: this story.*
- **Possible second layer (only if it stays one plain story):** the childhood boons, after Indra's thunderbolt struck his jaw (*hanu*, the name), when the gods made him unharmed by any weapon. Probably a side story: one line at most, or keep for another episode.

**Research correction (2026-11-26):** Hanuman asks for the boon at the farewell **after Rama's coronation** (CE 7.39), not as Rama leaves the world. At the departure Rama *holds him to it* (CE 7.98.24–25). And Indra had already given him death only at his own wish (CE 4.65.27). See 01-research.md.

## Watch for in research
- **The boon's exact words and place:** believed to be Valmiki Uttara Kanda, Rama's departure (to locate the sarga; IIT Kanpur text first). Who asks, who grants (Rama; check whether Sita's boon in Sundara/Yuddha also matters).
- **The seven-chiranjeevi verse:** where it comes from (series rule: "the traditional verse", unless a text is found).
- **Childhood boons** (Uttara Kanda, the Indra-Vayu episode) and the sages' curse that made him forget his strength: keep apart from the main spine.
- **Pop and devotional versions to keep apart:** Hanuman met by Bhima in the Mahabharata (Vana Parva; true, but a different text), present-day sightings, "lives on Gandhamadana", Tulsidas's *Hanuman Chalisa* and Ramcharitmanas wording.
- **Hindi names:** हनुमान, राम, सीता, वाल्मीकि, अयोध्या.

## Before storyboard
- **New visual domain:** Hanuman, Rama, Ayodhya have no approved precedent. Concept check (Stage 4) before the cast. Hanuman is shown with dignity: no cartoon monkey, a strong devotee with a gada (CLAUDE.md rule 2).
