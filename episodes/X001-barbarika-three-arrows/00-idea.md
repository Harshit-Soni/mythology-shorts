# X001: Barbarika: The Warrior Who Gave His Head

- **Series:** one-off (could join S01 later: some tellings make Barbarika a yaksha cursed to be beheaded by Vishnu)
- **Idea (one line):** Barbarika, Ghatotkacha's son, could finish the Mahabharata war with three arrows. Krishna, disguised as a Brahmin, tests him with a peepal tree, hides one leaf under his foot, then asks for his head as a gift. Barbarika agrees, and watches the whole war from a hilltop.
- **Why it will work:** a huge "what if" (one warrior, three arrows, war over) plus a twist (God asks for his head and he says yes). It ends on a living tradition: he is worshipped today as Khatu Shyam.
- **Carried over from:** the "Video styling for chat" session (OpenMontage): a 15 s reel with 7 beats (cliff, quiver, challenge, arrow through the leaves, foot and eyes, the ask, the watcher). This episode expands it to ~60 s and builds it with this pipeline: SVG puppets rendered in Remotion, not Seedance clips.
- **Accuracy flag:** Barbarika is **not** in the BORI critical edition of the Mahabharata. The story comes from the Skanda Purana (Kaumarika Khanda) and from Rajasthani/folk tradition (Khatu Shyam). The script must say so ("in later tellings..."), and research must separate the Purana version from the folk and TV versions.
- **Created:** 2026-11-08

**Decision (2026-11-08):** tell the **Khatu Shyam story straight** (three arrows, the losing-side vow, the leaf test, the head given, the head watching the war, Khatu Shyam). The Skanda Purana version goes in the description and pinned comment. The first cut, which switched between versions, was confusing.
