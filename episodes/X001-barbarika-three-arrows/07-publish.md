## Comment replies to have ready
- *"Barbarika is in the Mahabharata!"* → Not in the Vyasa text or the BORI critical edition. His story is in the Skanda Purana (Kaumarika Khanda 60–66) and in regional traditions: Khatu (Rajasthan), Odisha (as Belalasena) and others.
- *"Krishna was cruel!"* → In the Khatu telling, Barbarika's vow to always help the losing side would have kept switching the war until both armies were destroyed. He gave his head willingly, and Krishna blessed him to be worshipped by his own name.
- *"Only Krishna's Sudarshana chakra fought the war"* → A popular telling. In the Skanda Purana the head sees one figure, half Shiva and half Vishnu (Hari-Hara).
- *"It was a banyan, not a peepal"* → The katha doesn't name the tree; tellings differ (Pattanaik says banyan).
