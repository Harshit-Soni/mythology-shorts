## 1. `khatu-shrine`: the Khatu Shyam image in its shrine
**Variations to ask for:**
- **a.** Close and frontal: the sacred head image fills the middle third; a marble arch frames it; garlands and lamps.
- **b.** Wider: the sanctum doorway with carved jharokha panels, the image smaller and glowing in the dark interior, lamps in the foreground.
- **c.** Folk-art icon: the image inside a Pattachitra-style roundel with a petal border, flat gold background, lamps either side.

**Rules:**
- **Architecture:** Rajasthani temple style, with white marble, a scalloped (cusped) arch, jharokha balconies and carved pillars. **No onion domes and no pointed Islamic arches** (S01E01 lesson: name the architecture rule).
- **The image:** a dark stone face with large eyes and a tilak, a gold mukut with a peacock feather, heavy rose and marigold garlands. A respectful folk-art **interpretation**, not a copy of any temple photograph.
- **No text**, no crowd, no modern objects (no electric lights, no signboards).
- Keep the top 25% calm (title area) and the bottom 20% simple (captions).

**References** (openly licensed only, e.g. Wikimedia Commons, for architecture *style*, not the murti itself): Rajasthani temple jharokhas and marble arches. **Never** Pinterest, other channels' art or devotional-poster scans.

## 2. `barbarika-head-sky`: the immortal head watching the war
**Variations to ask for** (wait for Barbarika's batch-1 approval and attach his design sheet):
- **a.** The face enormous and centred in the sky, translucent gold, curl-clouds swirling around the jaw, the battle tiny below.
- **b.** The face in profile, gazing down at the battle from the upper left; clouds form the hair.
- **c.** Folk-art style: the face drawn like a Pattachitra sun-face, inside a ring of flame-clouds.

**Rules:**
- **No neck, no cut edge, nothing anatomical below the chin**: only cloud. Serene, eyes open, divine (CLAUDE.md rule 2).
- It must still read as **Barbarika** (curly topknot, tilak, ear studs).
- Test at phone size over `red-sky-plain`: the face must read in under a second.
