# The Lost Gyaan: logo prompts for Gemini (Nano Banana)

Paste one prompt per chat in the Gemini app. Generate **2–3 tries of each direction**, then upload the ones you like and say which direction each is from.

**Tips**
- Start each prompt in a **new chat**, so earlier images don't steer it.
- If an image comes out portrait, reply: *"Same design, square 1:1 canvas."*
- Gemini adds a small ✦ watermark at the bottom right. The prompts keep that corner empty, so it can be cropped out.
- Gemini often misspells text, and Devanagari more so. That's why the **icon (section 1) has no text**; the words come in section 2, and if the lettering is off, I'll set the text cleanly myself.

## 1. The icon (profile picture): 4 directions

It has to read at 110 px in a circle, so: one bold symbol, centred, no text.

### Direction A: the palm-leaf manuscript and lamp ⭐
```
A minimalist logo icon for a storytelling channel called "The Lost Gyaan" about forgotten mythology and history. An ancient Indian palm-leaf manuscript (pothi) lying half-open, with a single small diya flame glowing above it, the flame's light revealing faint golden lines on the leaves. Flat vector emblem style with bold, clean shapes, gold (#E8B53A) and warm saffron (#E07A2E) on a deep indigo (#1B1A3A) background, subtle parchment cream (#F3E6C8) highlights. Centred, symmetrical, fits inside a circle with generous empty margin all around, readable at very small sizes. No text, no letters, no people. Square 1:1 canvas.
```

### Direction B: the ज्ञ glyph in a broken circle
```
A minimalist monogram logo. The Devanagari character "ज्ञ" (the first letter of "gyaan", knowledge) in bold, elegant gold (#E8B53A) calligraphy, centred inside a thin gold circle that is broken and slightly crumbling on one side, as if part of the circle is lost to time, with a few tiny gold fragments drifting away. Deep indigo (#1B1A3A) background, a soft warm glow behind the letter. Flat vector style, clean and premium, readable at very small sizes. Only that one character, no other text. Generous empty margin, centred, square 1:1 canvas.
```

### Direction C: the keyhole into the past
```
A minimalist logo icon: an ornate antique keyhole in gold (#E8B53A) on a deep indigo (#1B1A3A) background. Through the keyhole, a warm saffron-to-gold glow (#E07A2E) shows a tiny silhouette of an Indian temple shikhara next to a Greek column, as if peeking into a lost ancient world. Flat vector emblem, bold simple shapes, symmetrical, readable at very small sizes. No text, no letters. Centred with generous empty margin, fits inside a circle, square 1:1 canvas.
```

### Direction D: the ink-wash seal (matches the illustrated videos)
```
A logo emblem in Indian ink-wash and gold-leaf style: a round stamp-like seal with an open ancient scroll at its centre and a small flame above the scroll. Black sumi-ink brush strokes with gold leaf (#E8B53A) accents on aged parchment (#F3E6C8), a thin gold ring around the seal with tiny lotus and laurel motifs (India and the ancient West). Elegant, timeless, readable at small sizes. No text, no letters. Centred with generous empty margin, square 1:1 canvas.
```

## What happens after you upload
1. I review each try: how it reads at 110 px, in a circle, in dark and light mode, and whether the colours match the brand.
2. I clean the pick: crop the watermark, square it, and export the profile picture (800×800), the YouTube watermark (150×150 transparent), the banner (2560×1440, safe area checked), the highlight covers (split into 5) and a small end-card logo for the videos.
3. The finals go into `channel/logo/`.
