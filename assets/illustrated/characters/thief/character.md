## Design lock (copy into every shot prompt)
Two men in rough grey-brown wrapped dhotis, bare chests, barefoot, rust-brown head cloths tied with the knot at the side, carrying knotted cloth sacks.
- **Thief A:** young, very skinny, messy black hair poking out, scruffy short beard, big nervous eyes.
- **Thief B:** older, stocky with a pot belly, bald under the head cloth, thick black handlebar moustache, a scar on his left cheek, sly look.
Tone: a little comic (the "crowd" register), never the same seriousness as the sage or the god.

## Sheet
Both standing, front · both running to the **right** with sacks over their shoulders · heads: A terrified, B sly, both caught and dejected.
