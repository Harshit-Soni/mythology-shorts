## Prompt used
Character model sheet of the sage Mandavya for a hand-painted mythology animation, redrawn in a new style. Keep his exact design from the reference image (Image 1): [design lock]. Do not copy the reference's flat vector look, beige background or labels. Layout on a plain off-white textured watercolour paper background, left to right: (1) full-body standing, front view, calm; (2) full-body three-quarter view facing right, chin raised, defiant; (3) a column of three head close-ups facing right: eyes closed in meditation, fierce and defiant, furious mid-curse with mouth open. Same proportions and design in every view, evenly spaced, nothing overlapping. Style: hand-drawn graphic-novel illustration, expressive black brush-ink linework with varied line weight and dry-brush hatching in the hair, beard and shadows, coloured with loose watercolour and ink washes. Skin is one flat warm wash plus one shadow wash; visible paper grain, wet-in-wet blooms, soft bleeding edges, a few small ink splatters. Semi-stylised heroic proportions, very readable expressive face with intense eyes. Dignified. No 3D render, no glossy cel shading, no smooth digital gradients, no text, labels, letters or watermark.
