## Design lock (copy into every shot prompt)
Mature, commanding god of about 45; warm golden-brown skin; strong jaw, heavy-lidded wise eyes; tall gold crown with a pot-shaped finial and a large red gem; white U-shaped tilak; heavy black handlebar moustache with short stubble; long black hair to the shoulders; gold drop earrings; white and cream garments with gold borders, **two crossed white sashes** over the chest and a gold waist sash; gold armlets with red gems; ruby pendant necklace; large gold halo disc with petal rays behind his head. **Not Yama:** no buffalo, no noose, no dark skin.

Differences from the SVG twin (accepted for the illustrated style): handlebar moustache and stubble instead of a short beard, a taller crown with a finial, two crossed sashes instead of one.

## Prompt used (v2)
Character model sheet of Dharma, the god of justice, for a hand-painted mythology animation. Image 1 gives his DESIGN (keep exactly): [design lock]. Ignore Image 1's flat vector look, beige background and labels. Image 2 gives the STYLE to match exactly (the sage's sheet): the same heavy black brush-ink linework with strong varied line weight, dense dry-brush hatching and cross-hatching in hair, beard, folds and shadows, the same loose watercolour washes, paper grain and ink splatters, the same level of realism and maturity in the face. Do not draw the sage. Dharma must look like a mature, commanding, ancient god of about 45: strong jaw, heavy-lidded wise eyes, a firm mouth, broad powerful shoulders, regal stillness. Not youthful, not cute. Layout: (1) full-body seated cross-legged front view, calm, hands on knees, halo behind; (2) the same seated pose in three-quarter view; (3) three head close-ups with halo: serene, stern, shocked (dignified, not comic). The gold halo and crown are painted as bare paper white with a warm yellow wash and a few tiny sparks. No 3D render, no glossy cel shading, no smooth digital gradients, no text, labels, letters or watermark.
