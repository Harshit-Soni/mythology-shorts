## Design lock (copy into every shot prompt)
Elderly Brahmin, slim and slightly stooped but strong; warm medium-brown skin (**never blue**: the disguise holds until the reveal); long white hair swept back into a small knot at the back of the head; a long flowing white beard and moustache; a **white U-shaped Vaishnava namam with a red centre line** on the forehead; a long tulsi-bead necklace; an ochre-orange dhoti with front pleats; a cream angavastram draped over the left shoulder; a plain knotted wooden walking staff; barefoot. Large, kind, knowing eyes and a gentle half-smile. Dignified and warm, never sinister.
