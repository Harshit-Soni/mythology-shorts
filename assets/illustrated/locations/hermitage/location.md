## Layout lock (copy into every shot prompt set here)
A forest clearing. Left: a huge old **banyan** with a twisted trunk and hanging aerial roots, and a **flat stone meditation seat** between its roots. Right: a small **thatched hut of mud and bamboo** with a dark low doorway. Centre: open earth with a **stone fire pit** (thin curl of smoke) and a **clay water pot** near the hut. Back: a misty **path** into darker forest. Dawn: pale rose and gold sky through the canopy, cream mist on the ground.

**Staging:** the sage meditates on the stone seat under the banyan. The thieves hide behind the hut (right). Guards come in along the path. The arrest happens in the open centre.
**Note:** the bottom ~15% of the sheet fades to blank paper. Shot prompts must ask for painting to all edges.
