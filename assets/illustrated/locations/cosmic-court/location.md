## Layout lock (copy into every shot prompt set here)
A vast open-air court floating among night clouds and stars. Upper right of centre: a raised stone platform with a gold **simhasana** (lion throne): a wide cushioned seat for sitting cross-legged, a seated lion on each side, round bolster cushions, a small footstool, and a tall carved arched back panel with a lotus and a lion face on top. Golden **scales of justice** stand on the platform to the left of the throne. **Glowing white-gold steps** rise from the lower left to the platform. Carved stone **pillars with lotus capitals** at the left and right edges, rounded arches at the top corners. Palette: indigo night wash, the throne glowing as bare paper with a yellow wash and gold sparks, rose-pink cloud edges.

**Staging:** Dharma sits on the throne (use `characters/dharma/sheet-facing-left.png` so he looks down-left). Mandavya stands or climbs on the lower steps, left half, facing right.
