## Layout lock (copy into every shot prompt set here)
A flat, rocky, empty **hilltop** in the middle of the frame. A **huge pale full moon** upper centre, a navy night sky with stars and torn grey clouds, a **faint crimson glow** on the horizon. **Dead leafless trees** on both sides of the hilltop. Lower left, far below: a **walled city** with warm lamp lights, a stepped temple tower and flat roofs. Foreground: dark boulders and dry grass.

**Staging:** the stake, when a shot needs it, stands on the hilltop centre and is only ever a **symbolic silhouette** against the moon (the sage seated in meditation on top, arms in his lap, never a cross shape). The king and guards stand on the hilltop.
